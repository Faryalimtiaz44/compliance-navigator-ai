The challenge

Compliance work is connected.
The information often isn't.

When requirements, proof, ownership and risk live in separate places, teams lose time reconstructing the picture instead of moving work forward.

▤

Fragmented requirements

Obligations are spread across documents, spreadsheets and disconnected team workflows.

⌕

Scattered evidence

Supporting documents are difficult to locate, connect to requirements and verify consistently.

◎

Unclear ownership

Tasks can lack a clear owner, due date or visible next step.

△

Limited risk visibility

Potential issues can be hard to compare, prioritize and follow through.

◷

Hard-to-track progress

Teams spend time chasing updates instead of seeing what is complete or overdue.

▧

Manual reporting

Creating a consistent status summary can mean assembling information by hand.

✦

One workspace. A more actionable view.

Compliance Navigator AI brings the moving parts into a connected demonstration workflow: organize requirements, link evidence, monitor risks, assign actions and report progress.

Project documentation

A clear brief for builders and judges.

Compliance Navigator AI is a proposed compliance operations workspace presented as a self-contained frontend prototype.

What it does

Organizes requirements, evidence records, risks, owners and actions into a unified sample workspace.

Problem addressed

Fragmented information and unclear ownership make compliance work harder to coordinate and report.

How the demo works

Switch dashboard views, search and filter records, add risks or actions, update task statuses and export CSVs.

Technology

Semantic HTML5, CSS3, vanilla JavaScript, inline SVG-free CSS visualizations and browser-native APIs.

Prototype limitations

No backend, authentication, persistent database, real file upload, AI model, regulatory feed or external integration.

Next development steps

Introduce secure persistence, role-based access, validated frameworks, evidence storage and governed AI assistance.

⌘

Technology note

Built with HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript. No paid services, API keys, package installation or backend hosting are required for this demo.
